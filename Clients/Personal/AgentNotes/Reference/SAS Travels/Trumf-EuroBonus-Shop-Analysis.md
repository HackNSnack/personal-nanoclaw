# Trumf → EuroBonus Shop Analysis

> Compiled 2026-06-18 from trumfnetthandel.no. All Trumf bonus rates confirmed via the Trumf Netthandel portal on this date.

---

## Methodology & Formulas

### Standard scenario (regular ticket redemption)

```
Step 1: Trumf earned  = Amount spent (NOK) × Trumf bonus rate (%)
Step 2: EB points     = Trumf earned × 13.5 (auto-conversion multiplier)
Step 3: NOK savings   = EB points × 0.10 (estimated value per EB point in economy)
Step 4: Effective cost = Amount spent − NOK savings
```

**EB point value assumption:** 1 EB point ≈ 0.10 NOK for economy redemptions (based on typical 30,000 pts ≈ 3,000 NOK round-trip Europe). This is a conservative estimate.

### 2-for-1 (Companion Ticket) scenario

With a companion ticket, you get 2 tickets for the points of 1, so the per-point value doubles:

```
Step 3 (2-for-1): NOK savings = EB points × 0.20
```

---

## Assumptions

- **All Trumf → EB conversion uses auto-transfer** at the 13.5x rate (not the one-time 10x rate).
- **Card payment separate:** These calculations assume you're already paying with a credit card. Stacking with SAS Amex Elite (earning 20 EB/100 NOK on top) is *not* factored in here — that would increase savings further.
- **"Opptil" rates:** Where a shop says "Opptil X%", I've used the listed maximum rate. Actual rates may vary by product category.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Lampegiganten (10.1%)** and **SillySanta (9.3%)** are by far the best earners — nearly 14% and 13% back respectively with standard redemption, and ~27% and ~25% back with a 2-for-1 ticket.

2. **Most shops cluster at 3.1%** (Blivakker, Scandic, Kitchn, Bakeren, Tilbords) — consistent ~4% back standard, ~8% with 2-for-1.

3. **eBay at 1%** is the weakest — barely worth the click-through.

4. **Stormberg, VPG, Helly Hansen, and Devold** all at 6.2% are the sweet spot for sport/outdoor gear — effective ~8.4% back standard, ~16.7% with 2-for-1.

5. **Always stack:** Use Trumf Netthandel click-through + pay with SAS Amex Elite (20 EB/100 NOK) for maximum earnings on every purchase.
