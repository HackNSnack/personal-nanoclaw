## Trumf → EuroBonus

| Method | Rate |
|--------|------|
| One-time transfer | 1 kr = 10 EB |
| Auto-transfer (recommended) | 1 kr = **13.5 EB** (35% bonus) |
| No fees, no minimum |

## Best Earning Rates (per 100 NOK)

| Method | EB Points |
|--------|-----------|
| Trumf Visa at Kiwi/Meny (2% + auto-transfer) | **27 EB** |
| SAS Amex Elite (all purchases) | 20 EB |
| SAS MC Premium (SAS flights) | 25 EB |
| Amex Platinum → EB | 20 EB |

## Key Portals

- **SAS Shopping:** `onlineshopping.flysas.com` — 2-20+ EB/100 kr
- **Trumf Netthandel:** `trumfnetthandel.no` — 1-15% Trumf bonus (= 1.35-20.25 EB/100 kr)
- **Poengportalen.no:** compares all of the above
- **KickBack.no:** cashback instead of points

## Card Stacking Strategy

1. Trumf Visa for dagligvarer (gratis, 27 EB/100 kr på Kiwi/Meny)
2. SAS Amex Elite for alt annet (20 EB/100 kr, 2 Companion Tickets)
3. SAS MC Premium som backup der Amex ikke tas (15 EB/100 kr)
4. Alltid via portal (SAS Shopping eller Trumf Netthandel) før netthandel
