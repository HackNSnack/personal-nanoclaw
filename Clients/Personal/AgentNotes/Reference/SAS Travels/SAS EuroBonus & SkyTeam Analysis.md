# SAS EuroBonus & SkyTeam — Asia/NZ Travel Analysis

> Research conducted June 2026 for a multi-destination trip to Japan, New Zealand, and Vietnam using only SkyTeam alliance airlines funded by SAS EuroBonus points.

## SkyTeam Member Airlines (relevant to Asia/NZ)

| Airline | Hub(s) | Relevance to OSL→Asia/NZ |
|---------|--------|--------------------------|
| **SAS** | CPH, OSL, ARN | Home carrier. OSL→CPH feed. Only Tokyo Haneda in Asia (from CPH). |
| **Air France** | CDG (Paris) | Routes to Japan, Vietnam, China, SE Asia. Key partner. |
| **KLM** | AMS (Amsterdam) | Routes to Japan, China, SE Asia. Key partner. |
| **Delta Air Lines** | ATL, JFK, DTW, SEA, LAX | Trans-Pacific via US hubs. |
| **Korean Air** | ICN (Seoul) | Critical hub for NZ connect. Routes to Japan, China, SE Asia. |
| **Vietnam Airlines** | HAN, SGN | Direct access to Vietnam. Routes across SE Asia. |
| **China Airlines** | TPE (Taipei) | Routes to Japan, SE Asia, Auckland. |
| **China Eastern** | PVG (Shanghai) | Routes to Japan, SE Asia, Australia. |
| **Garuda Indonesia** | CGK (Jakarta) | Routes across SE Asia. |
| **XiamenAir** | XMN (Xiamen) | Routes throughout China and SE Asia. |
| **Virgin Atlantic** | LHR | Limited Asian routes. |
| **Saudia** | JED, RUH | Routes to SE Asia via Middle East. |

Source: [SkyTeam member airlines (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SkyTeam#Member_airlines), [EuroBonus airline partners (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBonus#SkyTeam_partner_airlines)

## Route Analysis

### To Japan (Tokyo)

| Route | Airlines | SkyTeam? | Notes |
|-------|----------|----------|-------|
| OSL → CPH → HND | SAS | ✅ | Only SAS SkyTeam option. SAS currently serves Tokyo Haneda from CPH. |
| OSL → CDG/AMS → NRT/HND | Air France, KLM | ✅ | Via Paris or Amsterdam. Most flexible option. |
| OSL → CDG/AMS → ICN → NRT/HND | AF/KLM + Korean Air | ✅ | Via Seoul. Adds a stop but opens more award availability. |
| OSL → CDG/LHR → US → HND/NRT | SAS + Delta | ✅ | Via US hubs (SEA/LAX/JFK). Long way but more options. |

### To New Zealand (Auckland)

| Route | Airlines | SkyTeam? | Notes |
|-------|----------|----------|-------|
| OSL → CDG/AMS → ICN → AKL | AF/KLM + Korean Air | ✅ | Primary option. Korean Air serves ICN→AKL. |
| OSL → CDG → TPE → AKL | AF + China Airlines | ✅ | China Airlines flies TPE→AKL. |
| OSL → CDG/AMS → PVG → AKL | AF/KLM + China Eastern | ✅ | China Eastern flies PVG→AKL (codeshare). |

No SkyTeam airline serves Auckland direct from Europe. Seoul (ICN) is the key transit point for NZ-bound SkyTeam awards.

## How to Earn EuroBonus Points

| Method | Rate |
|--------|------|
| SAS flights | 100% of miles flown (basic points) |
| SkyTeam partner flights | Varies by fare class and airline |
| SAS American Express card | 1 point/NOK (2× on SAS purchases) |
| DNB Mastercard EuroBonus | 0.5–1 point/NOK |
| Trumf / Rema 1000 | Earn points on groceries |
| Online Shopping portal | 1–10 points/NOK at 200+ stores |
| Hotel partners | Scandic, Thon, Radisson, Hilton |

## Key Considerations

1. **Award availability is limited** — SkyTeam partners release only a subset of seats for award bookings. Book 330+ days ahead.
2. **Fuel surcharges apply** — EuroBonus awards on SAS and SkyTeam partners can have significant fees/taxes, especially from Scandinavia.
3. **Best value** — Use points for long-haul premium cabins where cash prices are highest. Economy awards give ~0.5–1 øre/point value; Business can give 2–4× that.
4. **Multi-city awards** — EuroBonus allows stopovers. You can potentially visit multiple destinations on a single award, but complex routings may count as multiple awards.
5. **Dynamic pricing** — SAS has moved toward dynamic pricing. The chart above is a baseline; actual costs vary by demand and availability.
6. **Book early** — Award seats are released ~330–360 days before departure. Set alerts.

## Feasibility Summary

**✅ Possible, with planning:**

- A pure SkyTeam-only trip covering Japan, Vietnam, and New Zealand **can** be funded entirely with EuroBonus points.
- Required points: **~250,000–350,000** for two people in Economy.
- The hardest leg is New Zealand — only **Korean Air** (via Seoul) and **China Airlines** (via Taipei) are SkyTeam carriers serving Auckland.
- Ideal routing: OSL → CDG/AMS → HND (Japan) → HND → ICN → AKL (NZ) → AKL → ICN → CDG → OSL — several options to construct a loop.
- Korean Air's ICN hub is the single most important node for making this work.

**To make it happen:**
1. Accumulate 300,000+ EuroBonus points
2. Search award availability on SAS, AF/KLM, Korean Air websites
3. Be flexible with dates (±1 week)
4. Book as early as possible (330 days out)
5. Consider flying Economy on shorter legs and Business on long-haul segments for a better experience
