# Grocery Store Price Comparison with EuroBonus

> How much does your grocery store *really* cost when you factor in EuroBonus earnings? Compare NorgesGruppen (Trumf) vs Coop vs Rema 1000.

---

## Methodology

Given a 100 NOK purchase of the *same item*, the "effective price" accounts for the value of EuroBonus points earned back:

```
Effective price = 100 NOK − EB value earned

EB value = (Trumf-kroner earned × 13.5 + card EB) × point_value
```

### Assumptions

- **1 EB ≈ 0.10 NOK** (conservative economy redemption: 30,000 pts ≈ 3,000 NOK round-trip Europe)
- **1 EB ≈ 0.20 NOK** (with 2-for-1 Companion Ticket, point value doubles)
- **Trumf auto-transfer** active (1 kr = 13.5 EB)
- **SAS Amex Premium** (15 EB/100 NOK) for all card purchases
- **SAS Amex Elite** (20 EB/100 NOK) shown as alternative

---

## 1. NorgesGruppen (Kiwi / Meny / Spar)

**Trumf:** 1 % on all purchases → 1 Trumf-krone per 100 NOK

### With SAS Amex Premium (15 EB/100 NOK)

| Step | Calculation | Result |
|------|------------|--------|
| Spend | 100 NOK | 100.00 NOK |
| Trumf earned | 100 × 1 % | 1.00 kr |
| Trumf → EB | 1.00 × 13.5 | 13.50 EB |
| Amex Premium EB | 100 × 0.15 | 15.00 EB |
| **Total EB** | 13.50 + 15.00 | **28.50 EB** |
| Savings (standard) | 28.50 × 0.10 | 2.85 NOK |
| **Effective price (standard)** | 100 − 2.85 | **97.15 NOK** |
| Savings (2-for-1) | 28.50 × 0.20 | 5.70 NOK |
| **Effective price (2-for-1)** | 100 − 5.70 | **94.30 NOK** |

### With SAS Amex Elite (20 EB/100 NOK)

| Step | Calculation | Result |
|------|------------|--------|
| Spend | 100 NOK | 100.00 NOK |
| Trumf earned | 100 × 1 % | 1.00 kr |
| Trumf → EB | 1.00 × 13.5 | 13.50 EB |
| Amex Elite EB | 100 × 0.20 | 20.00 EB |
| **Total EB** | 13.50 + 20.00 | **33.50 EB** |
| Savings (standard) | 33.50 × 0.10 | 3.35 NOK |
| **Effective price (standard)** | 100 − 3.35 | **96.65 NOK** |
| Savings (2-for-1) | 33.50 × 0.20 | 6.70 NOK |
| **Effective price (2-for-1)** | 100 − 6.70 | **93.30 NOK** |

## 2. Coop (Extra / Prix / Mega)

**Coop bonus:** 1 % on all purchases → 1 Coop-krone per 100 NOK
**Coop bonus value:** 1 Coop-krone = 1 NOK at Coop stores (can only be spent at Coop)

Unlike Trumf, Coop bonus cannot be converted to EuroBonus. Its value is strictly ~1 NOK per 100 NOK spent (redeemed on future Coop purchases).

### With SAS Amex Premium (15 EB/100 NOK)

| Step | Calculation | Result |
|------|------------|--------|
| Spend | 100 NOK | 100.00 NOK |
| Coop bonus | 1 kr (= 1 NOK future discount) | ~1.00 NOK |
| Amex Premium EB | 100 × 0.15 | 15.00 EB |
| Total EB value (standard) | 15.00 × 0.10 | 1.50 NOK |
| **Total savings (standard)** | 1.00 + 1.50 | **2.50 NOK** |
| **Effective price (standard)** | 100 − 2.50 | **97.50 NOK** |
| Total EB value (2-for-1) | 15.00 × 0.20 | 3.00 NOK |
| **Total savings (2-for-1)** | 1.00 + 3.00 | **4.00 NOK** |
| **Effective price (2-for-1)** | 100 − 4.00 | **96.00 NOK** |

## 6. Practical Comparison

If you see the *same item* at these stores with different prices, use this adjustment to find the true cost:

| Store | Adjustment (standard w/ Amex Premium) |
|-------|---------------------------------------|
| Kiwi/Meny/Spar | −1.35 kr per 100 kr (subtract from price before comparing) |
| Coop Extra/Mega | −0.75 kr per 100 kr (Coop 1 % bonus + Amex Premium) |
| Rema 1000 | −0.00 kr (just card earnings) |

### Concrete examples

| Product | Kiwi price | Coop price | Rema price | True cost Kiwi | True cost Coop | True cost Rema |
|---------|-----------|------------|------------|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| 1 kg chicken | 80 kr | 75 kr | 79 kr | **77.80 kr** | **73.50 kr** ✅ | **77.70 kr** |
| Milk 1 L | 22 kr | 24 kr | 21 kr | **21.30 kr** | **23.40 kr** | **20.50 kr** ✅ |
| Bread | 35 kr | 32 kr | 34 kr | **33.80 kr** | **31.25 kr** ✅ | **33.30 kr** |

*Coop bonus is worth exactly 1 kr per 100 kr spent (redeemed at Coop), not convertible to EB.*

**Rule of thumb:** Kiwi can be up to ~1.35 % more expensive than Rema (same item) and still be the better deal. Coop needs to be ~0.75 % cheaper than Rema to break even with Amex Premium.

---

## 7. Key Takeaways

1. **Kiwi/Meny with Trumf + Amex Premium** is the most effective option — 97.15 kr/100 kr (standard), 94.30 kr (2-for-1)
2. **Trippel-Trumf Torsdag** is a massive boost — effective price drops to 94.45 kr (Amex Premium) or 93.95 kr (Amex Elite)
3. **Rema 1000** has no loyalty program — only the card earnings apply (98.50 kr/100 kr with Amex Premium)
4. **Coop** sits in the middle — its 1 % store bonus + Amex Premium = 97.50 kr effective
5. **The gap is small** in practice: only ~1.35 kr difference per 100 kr between the best (Kiwi) and worst (Rema) with Amex Premium
6. **Don't overpay for Trumf** — a 2 % price difference at Rema still beats Kiwi. Use the per-store adjustment to compare real prices.
