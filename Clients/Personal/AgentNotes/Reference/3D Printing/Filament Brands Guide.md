# 3D Filament Brands Guide

Source: Community consensus, 3DJake customer ratings, and collective experience. Last updated: 2026-06-17.

## Tier 1 — Universally Trusted (newcomer-safe)

| Brand | Best For | €/kg (approx) | Notes |
|-------|----------|---------------|-------|
| **Polymaker** | PLA (PolyTerra), PETG, ASA, PC | 15–25 | #1 on 3DJake — 4.7★ across hundreds of reviews. Excellent diameter tolerance, great matte finish on PolyTerra. |
| **eSUN** | PLA+, PETG, ABS+ | 13–20 | Community workhorse. PLA+ is legendary — easy to print, good layer adhesion, very affordable. PETG at 4.6★. |
| **3DJAKE** (house brand) | ecoPLA, ASA, PETG | 16–20 | Made in EU. ecoPLA Black: 4.6★ from 989 reviews. Great quality/price ratio. |
| **Elegoo** | PLA, TPU | 13–18 | Rapidly growing. PLA 4.8★, TPU 4.5★. Excellent value, especially on sale. |

## Tier 2 — Excellent, More Specialized

- **Overture** — Very popular in US, great PETG and PLA+. Solid all-rounder.
- **Sunlu** — Silk/metallic PLAs (gorgeous finishes) and filament dryers. Newer lines (Meta, Silk) are good; older clear/normal PLA had issues.
- **AzureFilm** — PETG Hyper Speed at 11€/kg on sale (5.0★). Polish brand, great value.
- **Extrudr** — Premium Austrian. Green-TEC PRO is an excellent eco-friendly engineering filament. Pricey (~45€/kg).
- **Fiberlogy** — Polish brand, specialist in engineering materials (nylon, carbon fiber). Well-reviewed.
- **ColorFabb** — Dutch premium, excellent specialty co-polyesters. Long-standing reputation.
- **Fillamentum** — Czech premium. Extrafill PLA has gorgeous unique colours.

## Tier 3 — Premium / Maker-Owned

- **Prusament** (Prusa's own) — Best tolerances in the industry (±0.02mm). Expensive, mostly sold via Prusa shop.
- **Protopasta** — US specialty filaments (metal-filled, conductive, glow, carbon fiber blends).
- **Recreus** — Best flexible filaments (Filaflex). Spain-based!

## What to Avoid (especially beginners)

- No-name Aliexpress filaments — inconsistent diameter causes clogs and failed prints. Not worth saving 2–3€.
- Geeetech — mixed reviews, quality control issues.
- Old SUNLU (clear/normal PLA) — inconsistent; newer lines are fine.
- Any filament without published diameter tolerance specs (±0.03mm or better).

## Shopping Tips

1. **3DJake** stocks only reputable brands; filter by 4.5★+ with 50+ reviews = safe.
2. **Aliexpress:** stick to official stores of known brands (eSun Official, Polymaker Official, Sunlu Official).
3. **Typical good prices** (€/kg): PLA 15–20, PETG 16–22, ABS 15–20. Cheaper from unknown brands = risk.
4. **Starting out:** buy Polymaker PolyTerra PLA — forgiving, great finish, ~15€/kg on 3DJake.
