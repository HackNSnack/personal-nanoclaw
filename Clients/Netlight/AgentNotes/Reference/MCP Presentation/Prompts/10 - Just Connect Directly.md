# Slide 10 — "Just Connect Directly"

## Prompt for Claude + PowerPoint

```
Create a slide following the design guidelines in [[Clients/Netlight/AgentNotes/Reference/netlight-design-guidelines]].

**Slide content:**
- Title: "Just Connect Directly"
- Subtitle: "The skeptic's argument"

**Body — Present the counterargument fairly:**

Frame as a quote or devil's advocate position:

"Why add a protocol layer? Just connect your LLM directly to your tools."

**The skeptic's points (as a list or cards):**

1. **Simpler architecture**
   "No extra servers, no protocol overhead — just function calls"

2. **Lower latency**
   "Direct calls are faster than routing through MCP"

3. **Tighter control**
   "You own the integration. Customize it exactly how you need."

4. **Proven approach**
   "Function calling works. Why fix what isn't broken?"

**Visual guidance:**
- This slide should feel like a pause — we're hearing the other side
- Consider a visual cue that this is a counterpoint: quotation marks, a different accent color (could use Yellow #FFF400 sparingly), or an icon (devil's advocate, question mark)
- White background
- Keep typography clean — let the arguments speak
- Don't make it look "bad" or dismissed — we're steel-manning, not straw-manning
- Proxima Nova font
```

## Purpose

Show intellectual honesty. The audience may hold these objections — acknowledge them before addressing them.

## Presenter notes

- Shift tone slightly: "Now, let's be honest. Not everyone's convinced."
- Read or paraphrase the points: "And these are fair arguments."
- Don't dismiss yet: "Let's take them seriously."
- Creates tension before the rebuttal in following slides
