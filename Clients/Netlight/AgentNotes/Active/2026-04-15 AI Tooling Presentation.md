# AI Tooling Presentation

Presentation titled **"It Might Change Your Tuesday"** — an honest, grounded take on AI tooling for a consultant/generalist audience.

## Details

- **Deck path:** `/home/mathipe/Prosjekter/Netlight/Presentations/ai-tooling/`
- **Duration:** ~20 minutes
- **Audience:** Netlight consultants, varying technical depth
- **Tone:** Grounded, slightly provocative, practical

## Narrative Arc

capability exists → starting is the hard part → here's how to start → here's where it leads

1. Open with the hype — then flip it: "AI didn't change my life. But it did change my Tuesday."
2. Acknowledge the ceiling IS real (complex tasks, autonomous workflows)
3. Reframe: the problem is the gap between capability and entry point
4. Entry strategy: start with tedious-not-difficult tasks (strategy, not settling)
5. Tooling landscape: overwhelming, no one-fits-all — personal fit > best tool
6. Personal take: skills, sub-agents, memory, MCP — failure story (Obsidian + MCP → wrapped in skill → use daily)
7. Turning point: one workflow, made yours, repeated
8. The ceiling compounds: complex work follows from simple starts
9. CTA: pick one thing this week

## Key Design Decisions

- "Tedious-not-difficult" positioned as an *entry strategy*, not a definition of AI's value
- Tooling landscape framed as "overwhelming by design" before resolving to "find your fit"
- Personal testimony frame ("I can only tell you what worked for me") avoids feeling like a product pitch
- Failure story (Obsidian + MCP → skills) makes iteration concrete and credible
- Ladder diagram is the central visual — introduced in slide 13, echoed in slide 15
